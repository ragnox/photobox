Add photos

Pick a folder of photos, or the folder where you extracted your Google Takeout zips. Photobox only reads it - nothing is moved or changed - and keeps watching it for new files.

Getting your photos out of Google
  1. Go to takeout.google.com, deselect all, then select Google Photos.
  2. Export as .zip (pick 50 GB parts to get fewer files) and download every part.
  3. Extract all parts into one folder - mixed-up sidecars between parts are fine.
  4. Add that folder here.
