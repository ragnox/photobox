Connect to Photobox

This device will be able to see and favorite the photos in the Photobox library on your computer.

To connect, open Photobox on your computer, choose Connect a phone, and scan the code it shows with this phone's camera.

To install Photobox as an app (once)

Your browser doesn't trust Photobox's own certificate yet, and it only installs apps from trusted connections.

  1. Download the Photobox certificate.
  2. In Android Settings, search for CA certificate, tap it, and pick photobox-ca.crt from Downloads. On a Pixel it is under Security & privacy → More security settings → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate.
  3. Check that its SHA-256 fingerprint matches the one Photobox shows on your computer under Connect a phone.
  4. Come back here and reload. Then choose Install app or Add to Home screen in the browser menu.

To use Photobox like an app, choose Install app or Add to Home screen in the browser menu.
